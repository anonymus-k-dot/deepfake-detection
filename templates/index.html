Architecture — How it works
01 — EYE

Texture — eye stream

A fixed Laplacian high-pass filter highlights eyelash and eyelid micro-texture before a ResNet backbone with Squeeze-and-Excitation attention amplifies artifact-encoding channels.

02 — NOSE

Structure — nose stream

A dilation-preserving CNN without MaxPool retains spatial resolution across the nose bridge, where GAN blending boundaries produce detectable warping. CBAM attention localises the distortion site.

03 — FACE

Symmetry — full face hybrid

A ResNet backbone produces 49 spatial patch tokens fed into a Vision Transformer. Self-attention across all patches detects cross-facial lighting asymmetry that local CNNs cannot capture.

04 — TEMPORAL

Motion — temporal stream

Absolute pixel differences between consecutive frames form a 6-channel motion tensor. A dedicated CNN analyses inter-frame jitter patterns, stripped of all identity information.

05 — FUSION

Fusion head

Four feature vectors (256 + 256 + 512 + 256 = 1280 dimensions) are concatenated and passed through a two-layer FC head with BatchNorm and Dropout before producing a single binary logit.

06 — DATA

Data integrity

Trained on FaceForensics++ with a strict 70/15/15 video-ID split. No frames from the same source video appear in both training and test sets, preventing data leakage.
